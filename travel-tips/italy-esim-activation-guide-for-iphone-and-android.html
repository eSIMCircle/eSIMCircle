Activating an Italy eSIM on iPhone and Android, in three moments

Activation is easier when you split it into three short moments: at home, just before the flight, and in the arrivals hall. Each has two or three steps.

Published September 21, 2025 · Updated October 10, 2025 · 3 min read

Moment one: at home, on Wi-Fi

Do this a day or two before the trip.

On iPhone, open Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM and scan the QR code from your eSIMCircle email. On Android, open Settings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM and scan the same code, or enter the details manually if the option is offered.

When the phone asks for a label, call the new line "Italy". If it asks which line to use for calls, texts and data, choose your home line for now.

NoteSave the QR email or a screenshot of it somewhere you can open without data.

Moment two: just before the flight

Two settings, both on your home line:

  • Data roaming off. This is what prevents roaming charges.
  • Mobile data switching off. On iPhone it appears as "Allow Mobile Data Switching". It lets the phone use the home line when the eSIM is weak, which you do not want abroad.

Download offline maps for your first city: Rome, Milan, Venice or wherever you land.

Moment three: in the arrivals hall

  1. Open SIM or Mobile Data settings.
  2. Select the Italy line for mobile data.
  3. Turn on data roaming for the Italy line.
  4. Open a map and wait a moment for it to load.

That is the activation. The phone may show the name of an Italian partner network and a roaming indicator; both are normal for a travel eSIM.

If moment three does not work

What you seeTry this
No signal on the Italy lineToggle airplane mode, then restart
Signal but no dataCheck the Italy line is selected for data and roaming is on for it
Data works, then stops on the trainTunnels interrupt signal; wait for open ground
Still nothing after a restartContact support with your order email and screenshots

Do not delete the eSIM while troubleshooting. A removed profile may not be reinstallable.

Troubleshooting questions

When should I switch my Italy eSIM on?

After landing. Install it at home, then select it for mobile data in the arrivals hall.

Why does my Italy eSIM show a different network name?

Travel eSIMs connect through local partner networks. Seeing an Italian network name is normal.

Will my home number still receive texts in Italy?

Yes, if your home SIM stays on for calls and SMS. Keep its data roaming off.
