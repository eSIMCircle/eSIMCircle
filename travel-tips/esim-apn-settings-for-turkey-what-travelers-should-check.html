Turkey eSIM APN settings: a field-by-field walkthrough

The APN screen on Android has a dozen fields, and it is tempting to fill them in. For a Turkey travel eSIM you almost never should. This walkthrough explains what each field means and when to leave it alone.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

First, the three settings that fix most problems

Whether you land in Istanbul or Antalya, check these before anything else:

  • The Turkey eSIM is your mobile data line.
  • Data roaming is on for the Turkey eSIM and off for your home SIM.
  • The phone has been restarted since landing.

If the eSIM shows signal and still has no data after those three, read on.

The APN screen, field by field

On Android, open Settings > Network (or Connections) > SIMs > Turkey line > Access Point Names, then tap the active entry. You will see something like this:

FieldWhat it meansShould you change it?
NameA label for the profileNo need
APNThe gateway the phone uses for dataOnly with the exact value from your instructions
Proxy, PortRarely used for travel eSIMsLeave blank unless told otherwise
Username, PasswordLogin for some networksLeave as provided
MCC, MNCNetwork codesLeave as provided
APN typeWhich traffic uses this profileLeave as provided

On iPhone, the equivalent screen is Settings > Mobile Data > Turkey line > Mobile Data Network, and it shows fewer fields.

NoteIf you changed something and data stopped, Android's "Reset to default" in the APN menu restores the original settings.

Why the APN is usually already right

When a travel eSIM first connects, it normally pushes its own APN to the phone. That is why the field is often already filled in. A filled-in field that matches your instructions is not the problem; look at line selection, roaming or signal instead.

Testing after a fix

Open a plain website, then the apps you will rely on in Turkey: maps, messaging, translation and any taxi or transport app. If a website loads but one app does not, the issue is with that app.

Contacting support

Send your order email, phone model, and screenshots of both the SIM settings and the APN screen. Do not delete the eSIM while troubleshooting; a removed profile may not be reusable.

Troubleshooting questions

Which APN fields should I fill in for a Turkey eSIM?

Usually none. If the APN field is blank, enter only the exact value from your instructions or from support, and leave the other fields as they are.

My Turkey eSIM shows signal but no data. Is it the APN?

Check the data line, roaming and a restart first. Those fix most cases.

How do I undo an APN change on Android?

Open Access Point Names and choose Reset to default, then restart the phone.
