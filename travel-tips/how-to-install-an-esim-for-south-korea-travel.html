How to install an eSIM for South Korea travel, and the apps to set up with it

Installing the eSIM is the easy part. The bigger surprise for many visitors is that Google Maps gives limited directions in South Korea, so the apps you install alongside the eSIM matter just as much.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Install the eSIM at home

On stable Wi-Fi, a day or two before the trip:

  1. iPhone: Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM. Android: Settings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM.
  2. Scan the QR code from your eSIMCircle email, or use manual entry with the SM-DP+ address and activation code.
  3. Label the line "Korea".
  4. Keep your home SIM as the line for calls and texts, and keep data on the home line until you land.

Set up the apps before you fly

Google Maps shows places in South Korea but has limited walking and driving directions there. Most visitors use a local map app instead.

NoteSign up for anything that sends a verification code while your home SIM is in range of home. Codes go to your regular number.

Arriving at Incheon or Gimpo

Select the Korea eSIM for mobile data and turn on data roaming for that line. Keep roaming off on the home SIM. Open your map app and check that it finds your location.

Korea's cities have dense mobile coverage, including on much of the subway network, so a connection that fails at the airport is usually a settings issue. Restart the phone and check the data line before anything else.

If you are moving between cities

Trains between Seoul and Busan are fast and popular. Save tickets as screenshots, keep a little data in reserve for station navigation, and top up or extend the plan if your trip runs longer than planned.

Troubleshooting questions

Does Google Maps work in South Korea?

It shows places but has limited directions. Many visitors use Naver Map or KakaoMap for walking and transit routes.

Should I install the Korea eSIM before I fly?

Yes. Install on Wi-Fi at home and switch it on for data after landing.

Will my home number still receive codes in Korea?

Yes, if the home SIM stays active for SMS. Use the eSIM for data only.
