Is your phone ready for an Indonesia eSIM?

Most travelers to Bali or Jakarta want two lines: the home number for codes and the eSIM for data. Whether that works depends on how your phone handles dual SIM, so that is where this check starts.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Can your phone run two lines?

Most recent eSIM phones support "dual SIM, dual standby": both lines stay on, one handles data. Some older or budget models only allow one active line at a time, or limit which slot can use eSIM.

To check, look in your SIM settings for options to choose a default line for calls, texts and mobile data. If you can set those separately, your phone supports two lines.

You seeWhat it means
Separate defaults for calls, SMS and dataDual SIM works; keep home SIM for SMS, eSIM for data
Only one SIM can be switched onSingle active line; you would have to choose one
No add-eSIM option at allNo eSIM support

Is it unlocked?

Carrier-locked phones reject travel eSIMs. On iPhone, Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock should say "No SIM restrictions". On Android, ask your carrier. Do this a week before you travel.

Getting the dual-SIM settings right

Once the Indonesia eSIM is installed at home:

  1. Label it "Indonesia".
  2. Set the home SIM as default for calls and SMS.
  3. Leave mobile data on the home SIM, with roaming off, until you land.
  4. Turn off automatic data switching.

After landing in Denpasar or Jakarta, switch mobile data to the Indonesia line and turn on roaming for it.

NoteGrab and Gojek are widely used for rides and food in Indonesian cities. Sign in at home so verification codes reach your home number without fuss.

Beyond the main islands

Coverage is strong in cities and popular areas of Bali. Remote islands, boat crossings and mountain areas can be patchy. Download offline maps and save bookings before day trips.

Troubleshooting questions

Can I keep my home SIM on while using an Indonesia eSIM?

On most dual-SIM phones, yes. Set the home SIM for calls and SMS and the eSIM for data.

How do I know if my phone supports dual SIM?

Check whether you can choose separate default lines for calls, texts and data in your SIM settings.

Does the eSIM work on boats to the Gili Islands or Nusa Penida?

Coverage at sea can be limited. Save tickets and maps offline before boarding.
