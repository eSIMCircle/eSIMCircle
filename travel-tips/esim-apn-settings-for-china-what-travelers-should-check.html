eSIM APN settings for China: what to check, and what to leave alone

When a travel eSIM will not load anything in China, the APN is rarely the first thing at fault. Here is the order of checks that fixes most problems, and why China connections work differently.

Published February 9, 2026 · Updated August 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Why China is different

Many websites and apps that visitors use every day, including Google services, WhatsApp and Instagram, are blocked on local Chinese networks. Travel eSIMs usually route your data back through a partner network outside the country, which is how they can reach those services. That routing is also why the phone shows the connection as roaming and why roaming must be switched on for the eSIM line.

If you want this to work without installing a separate VPN app, check the product page for a plan sold as not needing one before you buy.

Before you touch the APN

Work through these in order. Each one fixes more cases than an APN edit.

  1. The China eSIM is switched on.
  2. The China eSIM is selected as the mobile data line.
  3. Data roaming is on for the China eSIM.
  4. Data roaming is off for your home SIM, so the phone does not try to use it.
  5. You have restarted the phone, or toggled airplane mode, since landing.

If data still does not load, test with a plain website as well as an app. A page that loads in the browser while one app fails points to that app, not the APN.

Checking the APN

The APN (Access Point Name) tells the phone how to reach the data network. Travel eSIMs normally set it automatically when they connect.

What you seeWhat to do
The APN field is filled inLeave it. Compare it with the value in your order email only if support asks.
The APN field is blankDo not guess. Use the exact value from your installation instructions or ask support.
You changed the APN earlierReset to default (Android: APN settings > Reset to default) or re-enter the value from your instructions.

On iPhone, the field is under Settings > Mobile Data > [China line] > Mobile Data Network. On Android, look for Access Point Names in the SIM or mobile network settings for the travel line.

NoteCopying an APN from a forum or another provider is the quickest way to stop a working eSIM from connecting.

Things worth testing once data works

Open the apps you will rely on: maps, messaging, translation and any payment or ride app. In China many visitors also use local apps such as WeChat or Alipay for payments, so check that they open and sign in while you still have time to fix problems.

When to contact support

Contact support after the five checks above, with your order email, phone model, and screenshots of the SIM settings and APN screen. Do not delete and reinstall the eSIM; a removed profile may not be reusable.

Troubleshooting questions

Why can't I open Google or WhatsApp on a local Chinese SIM?

They are blocked on local Chinese networks. Travel eSIMs that route data through a partner network outside China can usually reach them.

Should I change the APN if data is slow?

No. Slow data is usually signal or network load. Change the APN only if support gives you a specific value.

Do I need roaming on for a China eSIM?

Yes, for the eSIM line. Keep roaming off for your home SIM.
