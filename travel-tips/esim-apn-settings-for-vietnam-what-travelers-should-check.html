Vietnam eSIM APN settings and first-day troubleshooting

Most first-day data problems in Vietnam are solved by two taps and a restart. The APN comes up only if those fail. This page keeps the steps in that order.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

The two-minute fix

You have just landed at Noi Bai or Tan Son Nhat, and the eSIM has signal but nothing loads.

  1. Open your SIM settings and make sure the Vietnam eSIM is selected for mobile data.
  2. Turn on data roaming for the Vietnam eSIM.
  3. Turn airplane mode on for ten seconds, then off.

Try a map. If it loads, you are done. The rest of this page is for when it does not.

Next: restart and check the home line

Restart the phone completely. While it starts up, think about whether your home SIM might still be grabbing data. If "Allow mobile data switching" (iPhone) or a similar automatic data option (Android) is on, the phone may fall back to your home SIM. Turn it off, and keep roaming off on the home SIM.

Then: the APN

Travel eSIMs usually configure the APN by themselves. Look at it only if the steps above have not worked.

  • iPhone: Settings > Mobile Data > Vietnam line > Mobile Data Network.
  • Android: Settings > Network or Connections > SIMs > Vietnam line > Access Point Names.

If a value is there, do not change it. If the field is empty, use only the exact value from your eSIMCircle instructions or from support.

NoteDo not copy APN values for Viettel, Vinaphone or Mobifone from the internet. Those are for local SIM cards, not your travel eSIM.

Apps to test once you are online

Grab is the usual way to book rides and food in big Vietnamese cities, and Zalo is widely used for messaging with local businesses and guesthouses. Open both once data works so any sign-in codes arrive while you are somewhere quiet.

What to send support

Your order email, phone model, and screenshots of the SIM settings and the APN screen. Say which steps you have already tried. Leave the eSIM installed.

Troubleshooting questions

Should I use a Viettel or Vinaphone APN on my travel eSIM?

No. Those settings are for local SIM cards. Use only the value from your eSIM instructions or support.

Why does my phone keep using my home SIM for data?

Automatic data switching may be on. Turn it off and keep roaming off on the home SIM.

Does a Vietnam travel eSIM need roaming turned on?

Yes, for the eSIM line only.
