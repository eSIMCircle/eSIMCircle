How to install an eSIM for United States travel

The US is large, and coverage that is excellent in cities can thin out on long drives through rural areas. Install the eSIM at home, then plan around the route.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Before you start

You need an eSIM-compatible, carrier-unlocked phone, home Wi-Fi and your eSIMCircle email.

A note for visitors who bought their iPhone in the US: iPhone 14 and later US models have no physical SIM tray and use eSIM only. Visitors from elsewhere usually have a phone with both a SIM tray and eSIM, which is what lets you keep your home number active alongside the travel eSIM.

Installing

iPhone:

  1. Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM.
  2. Scan the QR code from your email.
  3. Label the line "USA".

Android:

  1. Settings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM.
  2. Scan the QR code, or tap manual entry and copy the SM-DP+ address and activation code.
  3. Rename the SIM "USA".

In both cases, leave your home line for calls and texts, and keep data on the home line until you land with its roaming switched off.

Planning for distance

TripWhat to prepare
New York, Los Angeles, Chicago and other big citiesA plan sized for daily maps, transit and messaging
National parks and long drivesOffline maps for the whole route; coverage can be absent in remote areas
Several weeks or a long stayLonger validity, then top up or extend as needed
NoteDownload offline maps before you leave a town on a long drive. In remote parks, offline maps are the only maps you will have.

On arrival

Select the USA eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Open a map or your ride-hailing app before you leave the terminal. If nothing loads, restart the phone and confirm the data line. Leave the profile installed while you troubleshoot.

Troubleshooting questions

Will my eSIM work in US national parks?

Some parks have little or no mobile coverage on any network. Download offline maps and save directions before you go.

Can I keep my home number while using a US eSIM?

Yes, on a dual-SIM phone. Keep the home SIM for calls and texts and use the eSIM for data.

When should I install the US eSIM?

At home on Wi-Fi before your flight. Switch it on for data after landing.
