Is your phone ready for a Singapore eSIM?

Singapore draws travelers from across the region, and phone models vary more by country than people expect. A model that supports eSIM in one market may not in another, so check the phone in your hand, not the model name.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Why the model name is not enough

Manufacturers sometimes sell the same phone model with different SIM hardware in different countries. For example, iPhones sold in mainland China use two physical SIM slots rather than eSIM. Some budget Android models skip eSIM in certain markets. So the reliable check is on the device itself.

The on-device check

PhoneCheck
iPhoneSettings > General > About. Look for EID.
AndroidDial *#06#. Look for EID alongside IMEI.
Android (alternative)SIM settings. Look for Add eSIM or Download a SIM.

If there is no EID and no add-eSIM option, the phone cannot use a travel eSIM.

The lock check

A carrier-locked phone rejects other providers' eSIMs. On iPhone, look for "No SIM restrictions" under Carrier Lock. On Android, ask your carrier. Request an unlock well before departure.

The timing

Install the Singapore eSIM at home on Wi-Fi a day or two before you fly. Changi's free Wi-Fi is a good backup, but installing in a transit hurry is avoidable.

NoteLabel the line "Singapore" when you install it. On a two-line phone, clear labels prevent switching data to the wrong line.

After landing

Select the Singapore eSIM for mobile data, turn on roaming for it and leave roaming off on your home SIM. Singapore has dense coverage, so if data does not start within a few minutes, restart the phone and recheck the data line before anything else.

If your phone cannot use eSIM

If the device checks fail, a travelling companion with an eSIM phone may be able to share data by hotspot, depending on the plan's hotspot rules. Otherwise, the phone needs a physical local SIM.

Troubleshooting questions

Does my iPhone support eSIM?

Check for an EID under Settings > General > About. Models sold in mainland China use two physical SIM slots instead.

Can I check eSIM support on Android without opening settings?

Dial *#06#. If the phone has an eSIM chip, an EID usually appears next to the IMEI.

What if my phone is locked to my carrier?

Ask your carrier to unlock it before the trip. Locked phones reject other providers' eSIMs.
