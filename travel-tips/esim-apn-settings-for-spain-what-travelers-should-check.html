Spain eSIM APN settings, answered

If you have searched for "APN Spain eSIM", you probably have signal but no data. These answers go in the order most problems get solved.

Published July 2, 2025 · Updated July 12, 2025 · 3 min read

Do I need to touch the APN at all?

Probably not. A travel eSIM usually sets its APN automatically the first time it connects in Spain. Most "no data" problems come from three other settings, so check them first:

  • The Spain eSIM is selected for mobile data.
  • Data roaming is on for the Spain eSIM.
  • Your home SIM is not set as the data line.

Then restart the phone. That fixes the majority of first-day problems in Madrid, Barcelona or anywhere else.

Why does the phone say I am roaming?

A travel eSIM connects through a partner network in Spain, so the phone labels the connection as roaming. That is expected. Roaming must be on for the eSIM line and should stay off for your home SIM.

Where is the APN setting?

PhonePath
iPhoneSettings > Mobile Data > Spain line > Mobile Data Network
Samsung GalaxySettings > Connections > Mobile networks > Access Point Names
Google PixelSettings > Network & internet > SIMs > Spain line > Access Point Names

Menu names change between software versions. If the path does not match, search your Settings for "APN".

What should the APN say?

Whatever your eSIMCircle installation instructions or support team say. Do not use values from Spanish carriers' websites or from forums. They are for different services and can stop data entirely.

If the field is already filled in and data still fails, leave it alone and contact support.

I changed it and now nothing works

On Android, open Access Point Names and choose Reset to default. On iPhone, clear the fields you edited and restart. If you are unsure what the original value was, ask support rather than guessing.

NoteNever delete the eSIM to reset settings. A removed profile may not be reinstallable.

What should I send support?

Your order email, your phone model, a screenshot of the SIM settings and a screenshot of the APN screen. Say what you have already tried.

Troubleshooting questions

My Spain eSIM has signal but no internet. What do I check?

Make sure the eSIM is the mobile data line and roaming is on for it, then restart. Check the APN only after that.

Is it safe to edit the APN myself?

Only with the exact value from your instructions or from support. A wrong APN stops data completely.

Does a Spain eSIM need roaming switched on?

Yes, for the eSIM line only. Keep roaming off for your home SIM.
