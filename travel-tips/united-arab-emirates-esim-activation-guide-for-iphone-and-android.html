UAE eSIM activation guide: setting up dual SIM on iPhone and Android

Most visitors to Dubai and Abu Dhabi want two things at once: cheap data and their home number for bank and app codes. A travel eSIM alongside your home SIM gives you both, if the dual-SIM settings are right.

Published August 2, 2026 · Updated September 2, 2026 · 3 min read

How dual SIM works here

Your phone will hold two lines. The home SIM keeps your number for calls and SMS. The UAE eSIM carries mobile data. The settings below make sure each line does only its job.

Install at home

Connect to Wi-Fi and add the eSIM from your eSIMCircle email.

  • iPhone: Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM.
  • Android: Settings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM.

Label the new line "UAE" so you can tell the two apart.

Assign each line its job

SettingHome SIMUAE eSIM
CallsDefaultOff
SMS and messagesDefaultOff
Mobile dataOff once you landOn once you land
Data roamingOffOn

On iPhone these settings are under Settings > Mobile Data (Default Voice Line and Mobile Data). On Android they are under SIMs or SIM manager, as "preferred SIM" options.

NoteTurn off "Allow Mobile Data Switching" on iPhone, or automatic data switching on Android, so the phone does not fall back to your home line.

After landing in Dubai or Abu Dhabi

Select the UAE eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Open a map. Your home number should still show signal for texts if your home carrier has a roaming partner in the UAE.

Calling apps

Messaging works normally over data. Voice and video calls through some internet calling apps have historically been restricted in the UAE, so check the current situation before relying on one for important calls, and keep your home line available.

If data does not start

Restart the phone, recheck the table above, and contact support if it still fails. Do not delete the eSIM; it may not be reinstallable.

Troubleshooting questions

Will I still get bank SMS codes in the UAE?

Yes, if your home SIM stays active for SMS and your carrier supports roaming in the UAE. Use the eSIM only for data.

Which line should be the default for calls?

Your home SIM. Set the UAE eSIM for mobile data only.

Can I use internet calling apps in the UAE?

Messaging works over data. Some voice and video calling features have been restricted in the past, so check current rules before you rely on them.
