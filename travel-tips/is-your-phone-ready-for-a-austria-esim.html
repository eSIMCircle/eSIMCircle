Is your phone ready for an Austria eSIM?

Three checks decide whether a travel eSIM will work on your phone in Austria. A fourth, about battery and offline maps, decides whether it keeps working on a cold day in the mountains.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Check 1: does the phone support eSIM?

PhoneWhere to lookWhat you want to see
iPhoneSettings > General > AboutAn EID number
Samsung GalaxySettings > Connections > SIM managerAn option to add an eSIM
Google PixelSettings > Network & internet > SIMs"Download a SIM instead?" or Add eSIM
Other AndroidDial *#06#An EID listed alongside the IMEI

If you see none of these, check the compatible device list before buying.

Check 2: is it carrier-unlocked?

A phone tied to your home carrier will refuse a travel eSIM. On iPhone, Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock should say "No SIM restrictions". On Android there is no single screen; ask your carrier, or try the eSIM installation and watch for a lock message.

Unlocking can take days, so do this check early.

Check 3: is there room for the profile?

Phones can store several eSIMs, but some models limit how many can be active at once. If you have old travel eSIMs you will never top up again, remove them before adding the Austria one. Keep anything you might extend.

Check 4: will it survive a day in the Alps?

Cold weather drains phone batteries faster, and a phone searching for signal in a valley uses more power too. If your trip includes skiing or hiking around Innsbruck or elsewhere in the Alps:

  • Download offline maps for the area before you go up.
  • Keep the phone in an inside pocket.
  • Carry a small power bank.
  • Save lift passes, hut bookings and train tickets as screenshots.
NoteCoverage on the main pistes and in towns is generally good. Remote valleys and high routes can drop signal on any network.

When all four checks pass

Install the eSIM at home on Wi-Fi, label it "Austria", and switch it on for data with roaming enabled after you arrive. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.

Troubleshooting questions

How do I know if my phone supports eSIM?

On iPhone, look for an EID under Settings > General > About. On Android, look for an add-eSIM option in SIM settings or dial *#06# to see an EID.

Does cold weather affect my phone in the Alps?

Cold drains batteries faster. Keep the phone warm, carry a power bank and save maps offline.

What if my phone is carrier-locked?

Ask your carrier to unlock it before the trip. A locked phone will not accept a travel eSIM.
