Switzerland eSIM APN settings, and the roaming setting that matters more

Many visitors arrive in Switzerland from an EU country and assume their phone plan carries over. Switzerland is not part of the EU's roam-like-at-home rules, so the setting that saves money here is roaming, not the APN.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

The setting to check first: home SIM roaming

EU mobile plans usually include roaming across EU countries at home prices. Switzerland is not in the EU, so many EU plans charge extra there, sometimes a lot. Before crossing the border by train or landing in Zurich or Geneva, turn data roaming off for your home SIM.

Then let the Switzerland eSIM handle data:

  1. Select the Switzerland eSIM as your mobile data line.
  2. Turn data roaming on for the eSIM line only.
  3. Restart the phone if it does not connect within a few minutes.
NoteIf you are travelling Switzerland together with neighbouring countries, check the coverage list on the plan page. A single-country plan stops working once you cross the border.

When the APN matters

Most travel eSIMs set the APN automatically. You only need to look at it when you have signal on the Switzerland line, the line is selected for data, roaming is on for it, and data still does not load after a restart.

PhoneWhere the APN lives
iPhoneSettings > Mobile Data > Switzerland line > Mobile Data Network
AndroidSettings > Network (or Connections) > SIMs > Switzerland line > Access Point Names

If the field shows a value, leave it. If it is empty, use only the exact value from your eSIMCircle instructions or from support.

Mountains, valleys and trains

Swiss cities and main rail lines generally have good coverage, but deep valleys, long tunnels and high-altitude areas can drop signal. That is a coverage limit, not an APN problem. Download offline maps before scenic train journeys and hikes, and save tickets as screenshots.

Before you contact support

Have your order email, phone model and screenshots of the line settings and APN screen ready. Mention whether you crossed a border recently, since that can affect which network the phone joins. Keep the eSIM installed while you wait for help.

Troubleshooting questions

Does EU roaming cover Switzerland?

Usually not. Switzerland is outside the EU's roam-like-at-home rules, so check your home plan or turn home-SIM roaming off and use a travel eSIM.

Why does my Swiss eSIM lose signal in the mountains?

Valleys, tunnels and remote areas can have limited coverage. It is usually a coverage gap rather than a settings issue.

Should I edit the APN if data is not working?

Only after checking the data line, roaming and a restart, and only with the exact value from your instructions.
