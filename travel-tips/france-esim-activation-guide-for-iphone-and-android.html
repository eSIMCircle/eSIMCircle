France eSIM activation guide for iPhone and Android

iPhone and Android reach the same result through different menus. This guide puts the steps side by side so you can follow your phone's column and ignore the other.

Published August 15, 2025 · Updated August 15, 2025 · 3 min read

Installing versus activating

Two separate things happen. Installing adds the eSIM profile to your phone. Activating means using it for data in France. Install at home on Wi-Fi; activate after you land at Charles de Gaulle, Orly or wherever your trip starts.

Step by step

1. ConnectJoin home Wi-FiJoin home Wi-Fi
2. Open the menuSettings > Mobile Data > Add eSIMSettings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM
3. Add the planScan the QR code from your eSIMCircle emailScan the QR code, or choose manual entry

NoteMenu names change with software versions. If yours differ, search Settings for "eSIM" or "SIM".

The settings that cause trouble

Two settings catch people out on both platforms.

Mobile data switching. On iPhone it is called "Allow Mobile Data Switching". Some Android phones have a similar automatic option. When it is on, the phone can quietly use your home SIM for data. Turn it off while you are in France.

Home SIM roaming. Leave it off. Your eSIM is the line that should roam.

If the eSIM does not connect

  1. Wait a few minutes after landing; the phone needs time to find a network.
  2. Toggle airplane mode on and off.
  3. Restart the phone.
  4. Confirm France is still the data line with roaming on.

Keep the profile installed. Deleting it rarely helps and may mean it cannot be reinstalled.

Before you go

Save the eSIMCircle email offline and download maps for your first city. If you are heading out by RER or taxi from the airport, having the route saved means a slow first connection will not hold you up.

Troubleshooting questions

Should I activate my France eSIM before I leave home?

Install it at home, but switch it on for data after you land. That avoids using validity before the trip starts.

Do I scan the QR code again in France?

No. You scan it once to install. In France you only change which line is used for data.

What is "Allow Mobile Data Switching"?

An iPhone setting that lets the phone use your home SIM for data when the eSIM signal is weak. Turn it off to avoid roaming charges.
