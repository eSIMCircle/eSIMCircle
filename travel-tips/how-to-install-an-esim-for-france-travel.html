How to install an eSIM for France travel, and what the error messages mean

Installation normally takes a minute. When it does not, the phone shows a short, vague message. This guide translates the common ones into plain fixes.

Published January 28, 2026 · Updated July 6, 2026 · 3 min read

The normal installation

On home Wi-Fi, scan the QR code from your eSIMCircle email:

  • iPhone: Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM.
  • Android: Settings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM.

Name the line "France", keep your home line for calls and texts, and switch data to the France line only after you land.

When installation fails

Message or behaviourUsual causeFix
"Unable to complete mobile plan change" (iPhone)Unstable connection during downloadMove closer to the router or switch networks, then try again
"This code is no longer valid"The eSIM was already installed, possibly on another phoneContact support; do not keep rescanning
"Your phone is locked to a carrier" or similarCarrier lockAsk your home carrier to unlock the phone
No option to add an eSIMPhone model or region without eSIM supportCheck the device list before buying
Scanner will not read the codeScreen glare or small imageOpen the code on a larger screen, or use manual entry
NoteIf you are not sure whether an attempt worked, check your SIM list before trying again. Repeated attempts on a code that already installed can cause problems.

Manual entry

Your email also shows an SM-DP+ address and an activation code. Choose the manual option on the add-eSIM screen and copy both exactly. This avoids the camera altogether.

After it installs

  • Label the line clearly.
  • Turn off data roaming on the home SIM.
  • Save the eSIMCircle email offline.
  • Download maps for Paris or your first stop.

On arrival, select the France line for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. If it does not connect, restart before anything else, and keep the profile installed.

Troubleshooting questions

What does "Unable to complete mobile plan change" mean?

Usually that the download was interrupted. Check your Wi-Fi and try again, after confirming the eSIM is not already in your SIM list.

Can I reinstall a France eSIM after deleting it?

Often not. Contact support rather than deleting and rescanning.

Do I need Wi-Fi to install?

Yes, or another working internet connection. Installation downloads the profile.
