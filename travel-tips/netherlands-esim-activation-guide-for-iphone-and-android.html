Netherlands eSIM activation for iPhone and Android, with a fix-it ladder

Activation itself takes under a minute. This guide spends most of its time on what to do if data does not start, because that is when people reach for the wrong setting.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

The quick activation

Install the eSIM at home on Wi-Fi by scanning the QR code from your eSIMCircle email:

  • iPhone: Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM.
  • Android: Settings > Network & internet or Connections > SIMs > Add eSIM.

Label it "Netherlands". After landing at Schiphol, select it as your mobile data line and turn on data roaming for it. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.

The fix-it ladder

If data does not start, climb one rung at a time. Stop as soon as a map loads.

  1. Wait three minutes. A phone that has just landed can take a moment to find a network.
  2. Check the data line. The Netherlands eSIM must be selected for mobile data, not your home SIM.
  3. Check roaming on the eSIM. It must be on for the Netherlands line.
  4. Toggle airplane mode. Ten seconds on, then off.
  5. Restart the phone. A full restart, not just locking the screen.
  6. Check data switching is off. On iPhone, "Allow Mobile Data Switching" should be off so the phone does not fall back to your home line.
  7. Look at the APN. Only now. If it is filled in, leave it. If it is empty, use the exact value from your instructions or ask support.
  8. Contact support. Send your order email, phone model and screenshots of the SIM and APN screens.
NoteAt no point does the ladder include deleting the eSIM. A removed profile may not be reinstallable.

Getting into the city

Trains run from Schiphol to Amsterdam Centraal and other Dutch cities, and you can usually tap in and out with a contactless bank card or phone wallet. A working data connection helps you check platforms and connections, so try to have the eSIM active before you reach the station.

A note on labels

If you travel often, your phone may list several old eSIMs. Give each a clear name and remove only ones you are certain you will never use again. Keeping labels tidy prevents switching data to the wrong line by mistake.

Troubleshooting questions

How long does a Netherlands eSIM take to connect after landing?

Often a minute or two. If nothing happens after a few minutes, follow the ladder from step two.

Should I delete and reinstall if it does not work?

No. Work through the ladder and contact support instead. Deleted profiles may not be reusable.

Can I pay for trains from Schiphol with my phone?

You can usually tap in and out with a contactless bank card or phone wallet. Check that your card or wallet works before you travel.
