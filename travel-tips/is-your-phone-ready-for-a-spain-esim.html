Is your phone ready for a Spain eSIM? Start with the carrier lock

If a Spain eSIM refuses to install, a carrier lock is the usual reason. It is also the check that takes longest to fix, which is why it comes first here.

Published March 31, 2026 · Updated May 11, 2026 · 3 min read

What a carrier lock is

Phones bought on contract or with a carrier discount are often locked to that carrier's network until the contract ends or the phone is paid off. A locked phone will reject a travel eSIM from another provider.

How to check

On iPhone: Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock. "No SIM restrictions" means unlocked. Anything else means locked.

On Android there is no universal screen. Your carrier's account page or support line can confirm it.

How to request an unlock

Each carrier has its own process, but the steps are usually similar:

  1. Check the phone is fully paid off, or ask what is needed if it is not.
  2. Request an unlock through the carrier's website, app or support line.
  3. Wait for confirmation. This can take from minutes to several days.
  4. Restart the phone. On some phones, inserting a different SIM completes the unlock.
NoteStart the unlock at least a week before you fly to Spain. Carriers rarely speed it up for a departure date.

The other checks

Once the lock is sorted, confirm the phone supports eSIM: an EID under Settings > General > About on iPhone, or an add-eSIM option in SIM settings on Android. Then make sure the software is up to date.

Installing and arriving

Install the Spain eSIM at home on Wi-Fi and name it "Spain". After landing in Madrid, Barcelona or elsewhere, select it for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.

Troubleshooting questions

How do I know if my phone is carrier-locked?

On iPhone, Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock should say "No SIM restrictions". On Android, ask your carrier.

How long does a carrier unlock take?

It varies by carrier, from minutes to several days. Start at least a week before travel.

Can I install a Spain eSIM on a locked phone?

No. The phone will reject it until it is unlocked.
