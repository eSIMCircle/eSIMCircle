Is your phone ready for a China eSIM?

For China, phone readiness has two halves. The hardware checks are the same as anywhere. The app setup is not, and it is much easier to finish before you leave.

Published August 28, 2026 · Updated August 28, 2026 · 3 min read

Part one: the phone itself

Run these three checks a week or more before departure.

  1. eSIM support. On iPhone, an EID under Settings > General > About. On Android, an add-eSIM option in SIM settings.
  2. Carrier lock. On iPhone, Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock should read "No SIM restrictions". Otherwise, ask your carrier to unlock the phone.
  3. Software up to date. Install updates at home.
NotePhones sold in mainland China often use two physical SIM slots instead of eSIM. If you bought your phone there, check carefully before buying a travel eSIM.

Part two: the apps

Many apps visitors use daily are blocked on local Chinese networks. A travel eSIM that routes data outside China can usually reach them, and some plans are sold as not needing a separate VPN app; check the product page. Either way, set up China's everyday apps before you fly.

App typeCommonly usedWhat to do at home
Messaging and paymentsWeChatCreate the account and verify it with your home number
PaymentsAlipayRegister and add an international card if the app allows it
MapsA local map app, plus offline maps of your usual appSave your hotel in Chinese characters too
TranslationYour preferred translatorDownload the offline Chinese pack

Verification codes go to your home number, so keep your home SIM active for SMS throughout the trip.

After landing

Select the China eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Keep roaming off on your home SIM. Open a website and then the apps above to confirm they load.

If data does not start, restart the phone and recheck the data line before looking at the APN. Do not delete the eSIM.

Troubleshooting questions

Do iPhones bought in mainland China support eSIM?

Many mainland China models use two physical SIM slots instead. Check your phone's settings for an EID before buying.

Should I set up WeChat and Alipay before the trip?

Yes. Registration and verification are easier at home, with your home number receiving the codes.

Will Google and WhatsApp work on a China travel eSIM?

They are blocked on local networks, but travel eSIMs that route data outside China can usually reach them. Check the plan page for details.
