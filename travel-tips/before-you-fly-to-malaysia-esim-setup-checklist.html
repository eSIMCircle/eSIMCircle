Malaysia eSIM checklist: getting ready before the flight

In Malaysia, ride-hailing and e-wallet apps do a lot of the work. They all need data from the moment you leave the airport, so get the eSIM sorted at home.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Why setup matters on arrival in Malaysia

Kuala Lumpur International Airport is a long way from the city, and many visitors book a Grab ride or check train times as soon as they clear immigration. Both need mobile data. If the eSIM is already installed, you only have to switch it on.

The apps worth setting up at home

Ride-hailing apps can ask for a phone number and a payment method at sign-up. Doing that on home Wi-Fi, with your home SIM still receiving texts, is much easier than doing it at the kerb. The same goes for any e-wallet you plan to use, such as Touch 'n Go.

NoteKeep your home SIM active for SMS. Many sign-ups and bank payments send a one-time code to your regular number.

If you are visiting more than one country

Malaysia trips often include Singapore or Thailand. If yours does, compare a multi-country plan with separate single-country ones before you buy, and check that every country on your route is in the coverage list.

First connection

After landing, open Settings, choose the Malaysia eSIM for mobile data, and turn on data roaming for it. Open a map to confirm. If it does not connect, wait a few minutes, restart, and check the data line again before contacting support.

Troubleshooting questions

Can I book a Grab from KLIA with an eSIM?

Yes, once the eSIM is switched on for data. Install it at home so you only need to enable it after landing.

Will my bank's SMS codes still reach me?

Yes, if your home SIM stays active. Use the home SIM for calls and texts and the eSIM for data.

Is one plan enough for Malaysia and Singapore?

It can be, if you choose a plan that covers both. Check the coverage list on the plan page before you buy.
