Before you fly to Japan: an eSIM setup checklist

Most Japan eSIM problems are solved at home in ten minutes or not at all at the airport. Work through this list the week before you leave.

Published June 1, 2026 · Updated October 8, 2026 · 3 min read

A week before departure

These checks need time, because two of them may involve your home carrier.

  1. Confirm your phone supports eSIM. On an iPhone, open Settings > General > About and look for an EID number. On many Android phones, dialling *#06# shows the EID if the phone has an eSIM chip.
  2. Confirm the phone is not carrier-locked. On an iPhone, Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock should say "No SIM restrictions". On Android, ask your carrier if you are unsure. A locked phone will reject a travel eSIM, and only the carrier can unlock it.
  3. Choose the plan. Count days from the morning you land to the evening you fly home, not hotel nights. Japan trips often include long rail days with constant map use, so size the plan around your busiest day.

The night before

  • Save the QR code email somewhere you can open without data, such as a screenshot or a PDF on a second device.
  • Turn off automatic app updates and cloud photo backup over mobile data. Let them run on hotel Wi-Fi instead.
  • Turn data roaming off on your home SIM. This is the setting that prevents a roaming bill.

After landing

Switch mobile data to the Japan eSIM, then turn on data roaming for that line only. Travel eSIMs connect through a local partner network, so the phone treats them as roaming even though you bought them for Japan.

Open a map before you leave the terminal. If nothing loads within a few minutes, restart the phone and check that the eSIM is still the data line. Do not delete the eSIM to fix a connection problem; a removed profile often cannot be reinstalled.

Troubleshooting questions

Can I install a Japan eSIM before I leave home?

Yes, and it is the easiest time to do it. Install on stable Wi-Fi, leave the line off for data, and switch it on after landing.

Will my home number still work in Japan?

Yes. Keep your home SIM active for calls and SMS, such as bank codes, and use the eSIM for data. Keep data roaming off on the home line.

Why does my phone say roaming when I bought a Japan eSIM?

Travel eSIMs use a local partner network, so the phone labels the connection as roaming. That is normal and is why roaming must be on for the eSIM line.
