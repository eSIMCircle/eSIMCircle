FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers for travelers comparing Turkey eSIM options.

Can I buy a Turkey eSIM before I travel?

Yes. You can buy online before departure, install the eSIM on a supported unlocked phone, and turn it on for mobile data when you arrive in Turkey.

Do I need a physical SIM card for Turkey?

No. A travel eSIM is digital, so you do not need to swap a physical SIM card if your phone supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked.

Should I choose a daily plan or fixed-data plan?

Choose a daily plan for predictable high usage each day. Choose a fixed-data plan when you want a set amount of data for the whole trip.

Can I use hotspot with a travel eSIM?

Hotspot availability depends on the selected plan and device settings. Check the plan details before purchasing if tethering is important for your trip.

Topic-specific questions

Questions travelers ask about Marmaris eSIM

Is hotel Wi‑Fi enough for this traveler type?

Hotel Wi‑Fi helps with large downloads, but mobile data is still important for movement, transport, maps, and urgent communication outside the hotel.

Why does Marmaris eSIM depend on traveler type?

Different travelers use data differently. Families coordinate, remote workers call and hotspot, backpackers navigate often, and tourists use maps, translation, and tickets.

Should groups buy one eSIM or several?

Several eSIMs are usually easier when people split up. One hotspot can work, but it depends on battery, distance, and the person carrying the phone.

What is a safe data buffer for this use case?

Add buffer for arrival day, long day trips, hotspot sharing, video calls, and social uploads. Light travelers can choose smaller allowances.
