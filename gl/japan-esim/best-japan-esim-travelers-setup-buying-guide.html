Japan Travel & Connectivity Tips

  • Digital IC Cards Add a digital Suica, PASMO, or ICOCA card directly to your Apple Wallet or Google Pay before arriving. You can top it up using your eSIM data to tap through train turnstiles instantly.
  • Mastering Transit Apps Google Maps works flawlessly in Japan. It will tell you exact train arrival times, platform numbers, and even which specific train car to board for the fastest transfer.
  • Real-Time Translation Download the Google Translate app and use its live camera feature. Point your phone at menus, street signs, and store labels to have the Japanese text translated instantly on your screen.
  • Cash is Still King While credit cards and digital payments are widely accepted in cities now, always carry some Yen. You will need cash for local shrines, small street food vendors, and exploring rural areas.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep my physical home SIM active for SMS and calls?

Yes. Modern smartphones support dual-SIM functionality. You can configure the device settings to use your physical home SIM for incoming SMS (like banking codes) while utilizing the Japan eSIM exclusively for high-speed cellular data.

Does this profile include a Japanese phone number?

No. These profiles are highly optimized, data-only connections designed for internet access. To make voice calls, simply use internet-based applications like WhatsApp, FaceTime, Skype, or Messenger, which operate perfectly over the 5G data connection.

When exactly does my plan validity begin?

The validity timer (e.g., 7 Days, 15 Days) does not start when you purchase the plan or when you scan the QR code at home. It only activates the moment your device lands in Japan and connects to the supported local networks.

Can I share my internet connection via Hotspot?

Absolutely. 100% of the data allocated to these profiles can be shared via mobile hotspot. This makes it incredibly easy to connect a secondary laptop, tablet, or travel companion's device without incurring additional fees.
