FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers for travelers comparing Thailand eSIM options.

Can I buy a Thailand eSIM before I travel?

Yes. You can buy online before departure, install the eSIM on a supported unlocked phone, and turn it on for mobile data when you arrive in Thailand.

Do I need a physical SIM card for Thailand?

No. A travel eSIM is digital, so you do not need to swap a physical SIM card if your phone supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked.

Should I choose a daily plan or fixed-data plan?

Choose a daily plan for predictable high usage each day. Choose a fixed-data plan when you want a set amount of data for the whole trip.

Can I use hotspot with a travel eSIM?

Hotspot availability depends on the selected plan and device settings. Check the plan details before purchasing if tethering is important for your trip.
