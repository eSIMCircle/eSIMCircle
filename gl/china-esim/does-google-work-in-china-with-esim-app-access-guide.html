Should I download offline maps?

Yes. Offline maps are a smart backup even with an eSIM, especially for rural areas, underground transit, or weak indoor signal.

Can I use hotspot for app access on another device?

Usually hotspot depends on the plan and phone settings. It also increases data use quickly, so choose the plan accordingly.

How can I reduce app data usage?

Disable automatic cloud backup, save videos for Wi‑Fi, download maps in advance, and restrict background data for apps you do not need while traveling.

Which travel apps usually need mobile data?

Maps, messaging, transport, translation, email, booking apps, and payment-related confirmations are common. Video and cloud apps use much more data.
